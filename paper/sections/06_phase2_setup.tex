\section{Phase 2 Setup}
\label{sec:p2setup}

We used 30 Terminal-Bench~4.0 tasks: 12 development tasks for design decisions and 18 evaluation tasks for the
final comparison. The two sets do not overlap; 9 of the 18 evaluation tasks also appear in Phase~1. All 18 were
part of an early 37-task baseline sweep whose overall results motivated the v2.8 design; no setting was tuned on
them after v2.8 was fixed, and the evaluation used fresh runs.

The evaluation tasks were drawn with seeds 570--572. Each ran twice per condition with both models served through Factory's Droid
subscription. When that subscription ended, we ran each evaluation task once more per condition with GLM-5.3
Flash served by Z.ai and Opus by Anthropic. Both were reached through opencodex, a local tool we use to route
subscription access to any harness; it is not a model provider. A task counts as solved when every hidden test
passes. Cost is computed from logged tokens at public list prices, not from what the subscriptions cost us.
